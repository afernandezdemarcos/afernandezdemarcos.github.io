\title{Research}

\section*{Research Interest}

My research is focused on \textbf{directional statistics}, a field of statistics where data supports are different than the Euclidean. Specifically, I am interested in hypothesis testing problems with data lying on the hypersphere
\[
  \Sph^q:=\{\mathbf{x}\in \R^{q+1}:\|\mathbf{x}\|= 1\},\quad q\geq 1.
\]
My PhD research, advised by \href{https://egarpor.github.io/}{Eduardo García-Portugués}, aims to develop one- and $k$-samples tests of uniformity on the hypersphere.

Other of my interests include $p$-value combination methods and asymptotic theory.
